Consume the split clause parameters in the MariaDB formatters so an explicit spelling MariaDB cannot express is refused by name with `UnsupportedFeatureError` instead of being dropped: `MariaDBSequenceMixin` renders `CYCLE`/`NOCYCLE` and `CACHE = n`/`NOCACHE` and refuses `ORDER`/`NO ORDER`; `MariaDBTruncateMixin` refuses `RESTART IDENTITY`/`CONTINUE IDENTITY` and `CASCADE`/`RESTRICT`; `MariaDBRoutineMixin.format_drop_function_statement` refuses `CASCADE`/`RESTRICT`; `MariaDBDialect.format_table_constraint` and its shadowed `MariaDBTableMixin` copy refuse `[NOT] ENFORCED`, `[NOT] DEFERRABLE` and `INITIALLY ...`, and an unspecified constraint now renders the plain constraint instead of the `NOT ENFORCED` the old `enforced is False` branch produced; `format_begin_transaction`/`format_set_transaction` refuse `DEFERRABLE`/`NOT DEFERRABLE` and `WAIT`/`NO WAIT`. Probe answers were corrected from live measurement: `supports_constraint_enforced` is `False` (the old `version >= 10.2.22` boundary was MySQL's), `supports_restrict_view` is `True`, and `supports_materialized_cte`, `supports_with_data_clause` and `supports_transaction_wait` are `False`; `supports_truncate` is now consulted by the truncate formatter. The `MariaDBSystemVersioningSupport` declarations `format_system_time_as_of`/`format_system_time_between` were renamed to the `format_for_*` names the mixin implements, and the new `MariaDBNamespaceMixin` states MariaDB's `catalog`.`name` namespace shape once, with catalogue entries carried as schema objects rather than parsed strings.
