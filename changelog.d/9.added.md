Add `ALTER TABLE ... EXCHANGE PARTITION` support to the MariaDB backend. `MariaDBExchangePartitionExpression` (`expression/partition.py`, re-exported from the backend's `expression` package) names the partitioned table, the partition and the exchanging table, and `format_exchange_partition_statement` renders the statement; the validation clause carries one parameter per spelling (`with_validation` / `without_validation`), setting both raises `ValueError` at construction, a non-`Table` argument in either table slot raises `TypeError`, and leaving both unset renders no clause. The validation options are gated by the measured `MARIADB_VERSION_BOUNDARIES['EXCHANGE_PARTITION_VALIDATION']` boundary of 11.4.0: `WITH VALIDATION` and `WITHOUT VALIDATION` are refused by name below it and rendered from it, while the bare form stays available from 10.2, and `WITHOUT VALIDATION` is honored on the server rather than parsed-and-ignored. The `supports_exchange_partition`, `supports_exchange_partition_with_validation` and `supports_exchange_partition_without_validation` probes are declared on the `MariaDBPartitionSupport` protocol.
