Fixed the partitions listing: the statement no longer projects `SUBPARTITION_DESCRIPTION`, a column MariaDB's `information_schema.PARTITIONS` does not publish. Naming it made the server reject the whole statement (`Unknown column ... in 'field list'`), so `list_partitions()` and `introspect partitions` could not answer on any real MariaDB server. The record's `subpartition_description` field documents that MariaDB does not publish it.
