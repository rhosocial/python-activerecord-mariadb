Own the binlog and replication families instead of borrowing MySQL's: `MariaDBBinaryLogInfo`, `MariaDBReplicationInfo` and `MariaDBServerOverview` are this backend's types, fed by new `SHOW MASTER STATUS` and `SHOW SLAVE STATUS` nodes, with `binlog` and `replication` sections in the status CLI. Split per-table storage from `information_schema.TABLES` under `supports_table_storage_introspection`, leaving a view's slots `None`. Answer runtime metrics from `performance_schema.setup_instruments` (gated at 10.5), foreign-key dependencies, native `SHOW CREATE TABLE`, and profiles, samples and join coverage by exact aggregation; `unused-indexes`, `table-stats` and `index-stats` are refused with exit 2 because MariaDB lacks the `table_io_waits` summary tables, and `mysql.column_stats` was audited and rejected for profiles -- one usable field of five, empty until asked, and no collection time. Nine CLI types derive from `_TYPE_CAPABILITIES` with NAME and option checks, rows are parsed under the projected lower-case aliases, `version_at_least` guards the pre-connection column map, and `SequenceInfo.to_core()` projects onto the core minimum with `last_value` `None`.
