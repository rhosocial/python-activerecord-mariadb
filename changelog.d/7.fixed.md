Forward certificate material to the MariaDB driver. `MariaDBConnectionConfig` inherits `ssl_ca`/`ssl_cert`/`ssl_key`/`ssl_ciphers` from the generic `SSLMixin`, but `get_connection_params()` only emitted `tls_version` and the two verification flags, so mutual TLS was impossible and the paths were collected and then dropped. The certificate fields are now mapped onto the driver's `ssl_ca`/`ssl_cert`/`ssl_key` keywords (with `ssl_ciphers` mapped to the driver's `ssl_cipher`), and `from_env()` reads `MARIADB_SSL_CA`, `MARIADB_SSL_CERT`, `MARIADB_SSL_KEY`, `MARIADB_SSL_CIPHERS`, `MARIADB_SSL_VERIFY_CERT` and `MARIADB_SSL_VERIFY_IDENTITY`.
