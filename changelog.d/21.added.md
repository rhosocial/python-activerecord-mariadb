Added the neutral object-state introspection surface to the MariaDB introspectors: `get_table_state()`, `get_partition_state()`, `list_index_states()`, `list_constraint_states()`, `list_sequence_states()`, `list_schemas()`, `list_derived_objects()` and `item_exists()`. They answer with core's `TableState`, `PartitionState`/`PartitionBound`, `IndexState`, `ConstraintState`, `SequenceState` and `DerivedObjectState`, not with MariaDB-specific records. Partition bounds fold a table's subpartition rows into one `COMPOSITE` partition with `subpartition_count`, `MAXVALUE` and the `SYSTEM_TIME` `CURRENT` partition are reported, and `include_stats=False` leaves the counters out of the statement as well as the result.
